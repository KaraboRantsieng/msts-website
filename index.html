About Us

Your Trusted Security Training Authority in Gauteng

Mullin Security Training & Services is a PSIRA-registered training institution based in the Vaal Triangle, serving students from Sebokeng, Vereeniging, and surrounding communities. We are committed to producing work-ready, professionally trained security officers who meet the highest industry standards.

Our programmes cover all PSIRA grades from E through A, including specialized Reaction Officer and Cash-in-Transit training. Beyond the classroom, we provide hands-on job readiness support and PSIRA registration assistance to ensure every graduate has the best possible start to their security career.

With years of experience in the field, our instructors bring real-world knowledge that goes beyond textbook training — giving our students the edge they need to get hired and succeed.
